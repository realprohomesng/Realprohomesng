Biography

Innocent Abuchi Alajemba is a driven real estate entrepreneur and the visionary Founder, Chief Executive Officer of RealProHomesNG, a Lagos-based real estate agency. With deep roots in the property sector, Innocent began his career as a dedicated real estate agent, building a reputation for integrity, professionalism, and results-driven service.

Through hands‑on experience handling residential and commercial transactions, Innocent developed a nuanced understanding of the Lagos real estate market—from Ajah and Lekki to Sangotedo and beyond. Recognizing the gap between client expectations and industry delivery, he launched RealProHomesNG to redefine customer service standards in property brokerage, sales, lettings, and management. Under his leadership, RealProHomesNG has become a trusted destination for individuals, families, and investors seeking property solutions in Nigeria’s economic heartland. The company offers a full spectrum of services including residential sales, commercial leasing, property management, and real estate investment advisory—all backed by a firm commitment to transparency, professionalism, and client satisfaction.

Innocent stewards a team of licensed real estate professionals who are passionate about guiding clients through every stage of the property journey—from valuation to negotiation and closing. Their focus on personalized service and attention to detail has earned the firm a proven track record of successful transactions throughout Lagos State.
He attributes the company’s growth to foundational values of trust, innovation, and consistency. In a public message to stakeholders dated January 24, 2025, Innocent expressed gratitude for client confidence and reaffirmed RealProHomesNG’s mission to turn real estate aspirations into reality, exceeding expectations at every turn.
Looking forward, Innocent aims to expand RealProHomesNG’s offerings to include development partnerships, scalable investment products, and smart digital tools tailored to Nigeria’s evolving housing market. His ambition is to help clients—not just acquire property but also build generational wealth through real estate investments.

In sum, Innocent Abuchi Alajemba blends deep market expertise with entrepreneurial energy. From agent to CEO, his journey illustrates a steadfast commitment to raising industry standards and empowering clients across Lagos with accessible, trustworthy, and transformative real estate solutions.
